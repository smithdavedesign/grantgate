What grantgate is

grantgate is a browser extension, in beta, that stops risky “Sign in with Google” requests until you confirm them, labels the permissions they ask for, and keeps an encrypted record of sign-ins in your browser. It reduces risk; it can't remove it. Google still shows its own consent screen, and you or your staff still decide whether to click Allow there.
