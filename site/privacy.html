Privacy

Last updated 25 October 2026.

The extension

The grantgate extension has no server. Your sign-in record, your passphrase-protected key and your allow and block lists are kept in your browser's extension storage. Nothing leaves your browser: grantgate never sees your Google account, your tokens, your email or which sites you use.

This website and the waitlist

If you join the waitlist, we store:

  • your email address;
  • your role and team size, if you choose to tell us;
  • whether you ticked “happy to do a 15-minute call”;
  • when you joined.

We use these only to tell you when grantgate is ready and, if you ticked the box, to invite you to a call. We don't sell or share them, and we never add you to anyone else's list.

The waitlist is kept in a Postgres database run by Neon. The website is hosted by Vercel, which keeps the standard request logs any web host keeps. There are no analytics, no tracking cookies and no ads on this site.

We keep your details until you remove yourself, or for 12 months after grantgate launches, whichever comes first.

Payments

If you pre-order the founding plan, Stripe handles the payment. We receive your name, email address and whether you have paid; we never see your card number.

Remove yourself

Enter the address you signed up with and it is deleted straight away. You'll see the same message whether or not it was on the list.

For anything else, reply to any email you've had from grantgate.
